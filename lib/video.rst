=======
 Video
=======

NSL has a full-featured DVI and HDMI implementation. HDMI supports
in-band audio and other metadata through data islands.

What a frame looks like, and how pixels travel between blocks, is
common to every wire protocol carrying a raster and lives in
nsl_video.
